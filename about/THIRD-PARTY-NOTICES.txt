Autopilot for Delphi — Third-Party Notices
==========================================

This product includes 4 units derived from the following third-party project.


Delphi-MCP-Server
-----------------

Four MCP-server units are derived from the Delphi-MCP-Server project by GDK
Software:

  - the JSON Schema generator (builds a tool's inputSchema from a params class
    via RTTI),
  - the tool-parameter deserializer (fills a params class from the incoming
    JSON via RTTI),
  - the parameter-annotation attributes ([Optional], [SchemaDescription]), and
  - the tool-base scaffolding (the IMCPTool interface and its generic base).

The originals come from:

    https://github.com/GDKsoftware/Delphi-MCP-Server
