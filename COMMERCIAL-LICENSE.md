## Who needs a commercial license

You need a paid commercial license if your use is **commercial** — anything done by or for a business, or with an anticipated commercial application. In practice:

- Using it at your job, or on a paid client project.
- Using it inside a product or service you sell.
- Any use by a company (see *Your company* in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the exact definition).

You do **not** need to pay — it stays free — for:

- Personal projects, study, research, hobby work.
- Use by a charity, school, public research / health / safety organization, or government.

The dividing line is **commercial vs. noncommercial use**, not *"individual vs. company"*. A freelancer doing paid work is commercial; a company evaluating it for a genuinely noncommercial purpose is not.
